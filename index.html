From idea to trading

The same four steps every time.

  1. 1

    Name it

    Pick a name, a ticker and a logo. They are written onto the coin itself and locked, so nobody can swap them later.

  2. 2

    Set the supply

    Your coins are created once. Then minting is switched off for good, so no one can ever make more.

  3. 3

    Choose the fee

    Pick 1%, 3% or 5%. It is charged on every buy and every sell, it is set when the coin is made, and it can never be changed.

  4. 4

    Open trading

    Your coins go into a trading pool and the liquidity is locked permanently. An opening fee starts high and falls over the first minutes to slow down bots.
